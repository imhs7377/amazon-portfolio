02 / Case studies

Strategy translated into numbers.

Selected private-label results from a broader track record across 10+ managed accounts, supported by hands-on experience in both private label and wholesale.

The challenge

A healthy-volume account needed to keep scaling without letting ad spend outrun sales—beating the prior year’s unit count well before year-end while holding ACoS steady.

The strategy

Separated Sponsored Products into discovery and performance layers: broad-match campaigns captured new search terms, while proven queries graduated into exact-match campaigns. Non-converting terms were negated, keyword and product-target bids were adjusted against placement-level CVR, and Top of Search or Product Page multipliers were applied only where the conversion data justified them. Budget moved toward profitable ASIN–keyword pairs while ACoS, TACoS, CTR, CVR, and organic rank were monitored together.
